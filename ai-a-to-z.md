AI: A-to-Z makes important artificial intelligence concepts accessible without requiring programming or advanced mathematics.

Created and presented by **Dr. Sonali Tamhankar**, the series uses short, approachable lessons to build practical AI literacy—one concept and one letter at a time.

## 2026: The Foundational Series

Twenty-six concise lessons provide a shared foundation for understanding artificial intelligence. The series is designed for healthcare professionals, researchers, leaders, students, and curious members of the public.
